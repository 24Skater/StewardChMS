# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

In the interest of fostering an open, welcoming, and Christ-honoring community, we as contributors and maintainers of **StewardChMS** pledge to make participation in this project a respectful and constructive experience for everyone.

This project exists to serve churches and is built on principles of integrity, humility, respect, and stewardship.

---

## ✝️ Our Values

While StewardChMS is created as an expression of faith in **the Lord Jesus Christ**, all contributors are welcome.

We seek to reflect values such as:
- Love and respect for others
- Honesty and transparency
- Patience and humility
- Accountability and responsibility
- Excellence in work

> “Let all that you do be done in love.”  
> — 1 Corinthians 16:14

---

## Expected Behavior

We expect all participants to:

- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions
- Welcome differing viewpoints and experiences
- Give and receive constructive feedback graciously
- Assume good intentions
- Focus on what is best for the community and the project
- Take responsibility for mistakes and seek resolution

---

## Unacceptable Behavior

The following behaviors are not tolerated:

- Harassment, discrimination, or hateful conduct of any kind
- Personal attacks or insulting language
- Trolling, spamming, or disruptive behavior
- Publishing private or confidential information
- Deliberate attempts to undermine or sabotage the project

---

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies to all project spaces, including:
- GitHub repositories
- Issues and pull requests
- Discussions and community forums
- Direct communications related to the project

---

## Enforcement

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing standards of acceptable behavior.

Consequences for unacceptable behavior may include:
- A warning
- Temporary suspension from the project
- Permanent removal from participation

Maintainers will act in good faith and with fairness when addressing concerns.

---

## Reporting Issues

If you experience or witness behavior that violates this Code of Conduct, please report it privately to the project maintainer.

All reports will be reviewed promptly and handled with discretion.

---

## Final Word

StewardChMS exists to serve the Church and honor God through faithful stewardship and collaboration.

We ask all participants to contribute with grace, professionalism, and respect.

> “Whatever you do, work at it with all your heart, as working for the Lord, not for human masters.”  
> — Colossians 3:23
